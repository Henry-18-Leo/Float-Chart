About ARGO floats

A short guide for first-time users.

What is a float?

A small robot that drifts in the ocean, dives to 2,000 m every 10 days and measures temperature and salinity on the way up.

Why does it matter?

The data helps track climate change, monsoons and cyclones.

How to use FloatChat

Type a question in Chat, then open Map or Charts to explore the result.

Where is the data from?

The mockup uses sample values. The real version will connect to ARGO data through the backend.
